NEPSE Brokerage Commission Fees Nepal 2026: Complete Guide

📅 Published: June 8, 2026 ⏱️ Reading Time: 8 minutes 👤 NEPSE Calculator Nepal 📧 nepsecalcnepal@gmail.com

What is NEPSE Brokerage Commission?

Every time you buy or sell shares on the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE), your stock broker charges a brokerage commission fee. This is the broker's service charge for executing your trade on the NEPSE platform.

Brokerage is charged on both buy and sell transactions — meaning you pay it twice for every complete trade. The rate is set by SEBON (Securities Board of Nepal) and follows a tiered structure based on your transaction amount.

💡 Key Point: Brokerage is charged on the total transaction amount (shares × price), not on your profit. You pay brokerage even if you sell at a loss.

SEBON has set the following maximum brokerage rates for all NEPSE stock brokers in Nepal:

What is SEBON Fee?

The SEBON fee (also called the regulatory fee) is charged by the Securities Board of Nepal on every transaction. It is 0.015% of the total transaction amount on both buy and sell sides.

SEBON Fee = Transaction Amount × 0.015% Example: Transaction Amount = Rs. 1,00,000 SEBON Fee = Rs. 1,00,000 × 0.015% = Rs. 15

This fee is very small but is mandatory on all trades. Your broker collects it from you and passes it on to SEBON.

What is DP Charge?

The DP (Depository Participant) charge is a flat fee of Rs. 25 charged only on sell transactions. It is collected by your broker on behalf of CDS & Clearing Ltd (the central depository of Nepal).

  • ✅ Charged only when you sell shares
  • ✅ Flat Rs. 25 per script per day — does not change with amount
  • ✅ If you sell 5 different company shares on the same day → Rs. 25 × 5 = Rs. 125
  • ❌ Not charged on buy transactions

💡 Tip: If you sell shares of only one company in a day, DP charge is just Rs. 25 — regardless of whether you sell 10 shares or 10,000 shares.

Real Calculation Examples

Example 1 — Small Transaction (Rs. 30,000)

You buy 60 shares at Rs. 500 each = Rs. 30,000 transaction (falls in 0.36% tier).

BUY TRANSACTION: Transaction Amount = 60 × Rs. 500 = Rs. 30,000 Brokerage (0.36%) = Rs. 30,000 × 0.36% = Rs. 108 SEBON Fee (0.015%) = Rs. 30,000 × 0.015% = Rs. 4.50 Total Buy Cost = Rs. 30,000 + Rs. 108 + Rs. 4.50 = Rs. 30,112.50 SELL TRANSACTION (at Rs. 600): Transaction Amount = 60 × Rs. 600 = Rs. 36,000 Brokerage (0.36%) = Rs. 36,000 × 0.36% = Rs. 129.60 SEBON Fee (0.015%) = Rs. 36,000 × 0.015% = Rs. 5.40 DP Charge = Rs. 25 Total Sell Deductions = Rs. 129.60 + Rs. 5.40 + Rs. 25 = Rs. 160 Net Receive = Rs. 36,000 − Rs. 160 = Rs. 35,840 Net Profit = Rs. 35,840 − Rs. 30,112.50 = Rs. 5,727.50

Example 2 — Medium Transaction (Rs. 1,00,000)

You buy 100 shares at Rs. 1,000 each = Rs. 1,00,000 (falls in 0.33% tier).

BUY TRANSACTION: Transaction Amount = 100 × Rs. 1,000 = Rs. 1,00,000 Brokerage (0.33%) = Rs. 1,00,000 × 0.33% = Rs. 330 SEBON Fee (0.015%) = Rs. 1,00,000 × 0.015% = Rs. 15 Total Buy Cost = Rs. 1,00,000 + Rs. 330 + Rs. 15 = Rs. 1,00,345 SELL TRANSACTION (at Rs. 1,200): Transaction Amount = 100 × Rs. 1,200 = Rs. 1,20,000 Brokerage (0.33%) = Rs. 1,20,000 × 0.33% = Rs. 396 SEBON Fee (0.015%) = Rs. 1,20,000 × 0.015% = Rs. 18 DP Charge = Rs. 25 Total Sell Deductions = Rs. 396 + Rs. 18 + Rs. 25 = Rs. 439 Net Receive = Rs. 1,20,000 − Rs. 439 = Rs. 1,19,561 Net Profit = Rs. 1,19,561 − Rs. 1,00,345 = Rs. 19,216

Example 3 — Large Transaction (Rs. 10,00,000)

You buy 1,000 shares at Rs. 1,000 = Rs. 10,00,000 (falls in 0.31% tier).

BUY TRANSACTION: Transaction Amount = 1,000 × Rs. 1,000 = Rs. 10,00,000 Brokerage (0.31%) = Rs. 10,00,000 × 0.31% = Rs. 3,100 SEBON Fee (0.015%) = Rs. 10,00,000 × 0.015% = Rs. 150 Total Buy Cost = Rs. 10,00,000 + Rs. 3,100 + Rs. 150 = Rs. 10,03,250 SELL TRANSACTION (at Rs. 1,100): Transaction Amount = 1,000 × Rs. 1,100 = Rs. 11,00,000 Brokerage (0.31%) = Rs. 11,00,000 × 0.31% = Rs. 3,410 SEBON Fee (0.015%) = Rs. 11,00,000 × 0.015% = Rs. 165 DP Charge = Rs. 25 Total Sell Deductions = Rs. 3,410 + Rs. 165 + Rs. 25 = Rs. 3,600 Net Receive = Rs. 11,00,000 − Rs. 3,600 = Rs. 10,96,400 Net Profit = Rs. 10,96,400 − Rs. 10,03,250 = Rs. 93,150

How is Brokerage Calculated When Amount is on the Boundary?

The brokerage rate applies to the entire transaction amount based on which tier it falls in. It is not split between tiers like income tax slabs.

⚠️ Example: If your transaction is Rs. 50,001 — the entire amount is charged at 0.33% (not 0.36% for the first Rs. 50,000 and 0.33% for Rs. 1). The tier that your total amount falls into applies to the whole amount.

Transaction = Rs. 50,001 Rate = 0.33% (because it exceeded Rs. 50,000) Brokerage = Rs. 50,001 × 0.33% = Rs. 165 NOT: Rs. 50,000 × 0.36% + Rs. 1 × 0.33% = Rs. 180.33 ← WRONG

How to Minimise Brokerage Costs

  • ✅ Trade in larger amounts — higher tiers have lower percentage rates
  • ✅ Reduce trade frequency — every buy and sell incurs brokerage both ways
  • ✅ Consolidate purchases — buying 500 shares in one trade costs less than buying 100 shares in 5 separate trades
  • ✅ Use our calculator — know exact charges before trading so there are no surprises

✅ Tip: A transaction of Rs. 5,00,000 is charged at 0.33%, but Rs. 5,00,001 drops to 0.31%. Being aware of tier boundaries can save you money on large trades.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Is brokerage charged on both buy and sell?

Yes! Brokerage is charged on every transaction — both when you buy and when you sell. For a complete trade (buy + sell), you pay brokerage twice.

Q2: What is the minimum brokerage in NEPSE?

The minimum brokerage is Rs. 10. For transactions of Rs. 2,500 or less, a flat Rs. 10 is charged. For amounts above Rs. 2,500, the percentage rate applies.

Q3: Can my broker charge more than the SEBON rates?

No. The rates set by SEBON are the maximum allowed rates. Your broker cannot legally charge more. However, brokers may offer discounts below these rates.

Q4: Is DP charge Rs. 25 per share or per transaction?

Rs. 25 is per script (company) per day — not per share. If you sell 1,000 shares of one company, DP charge is still Rs. 25. If you sell shares of 3 different companies, DP charge is Rs. 75 total.

Q5: Is SEBON fee included in brokerage or separate?

SEBON fee is separate from brokerage. Your broker collects both but they are different charges — brokerage goes to the broker, SEBON fee is passed on to SEBON.

Q6: Do I pay brokerage on IPO applications?

No. Brokerage, SEBON fee, and DP charge apply only to secondary market transactions (buying and selling on NEPSE). IPO applications through ASBA are free of these charges.

Q7: What is the brokerage rate for online trading vs floor trading?

The SEBON-set maximum rates apply to all NEPSE transactions regardless of whether you trade online through TMS or through the broker's office. The rates are the same.
