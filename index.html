Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NEPSE calculations

NEPSE uses a tiered brokerage system: 0.36% for amounts up to Rs 50,000, 0.33% up to Rs 5,00,000, 0.31% up to Rs 20,00,000, 0.27% up to Rs 1 Crore, and 0.24% above Rs 1 Crore.

For individuals: 7.5% on short-term gains (held less than 365 days) and 5% on long-term gains (held 365 days or more). Institutions pay 10% flat on all gains. CGT only applies on profit, not on losses.

The Securities Board of Nepal (SEBON) levies a regulatory fee of 0.015% on both buy and sell transactions in the NEPSE market.

The Depository Participant (DP) charge is a flat Rs 25 fee applied per script, per day, on sell transactions only. It is collected by your broker on behalf of CDS & Clearing Ltd.
