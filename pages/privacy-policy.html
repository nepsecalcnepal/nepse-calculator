Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Home / Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

NEPSE Calculator Nepal ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website. By using our service, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Our calculator runs entirely in your browser — we do not require registration or collect personal information directly. However, we may collect:

  • Local Storage Data: Your last calculation inputs are saved in your browser's local storage for convenience. This never leaves your device.
  • Anonymous Analytics: If Google Analytics is enabled, we collect anonymized usage data such as page views, browser type, and country.
  • Contact Form: When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email, and message to respond to you.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve the calculator features
  • To respond to your questions and feedback
  • To analyze website performance (anonymously)
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use only essential local storage to remember your last calculation and theme preference. We do not use tracking cookies. Third-party services (Google Analytics, Google AdSense) may set cookies independently — please review their privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services

This site may use:

  • Google Fonts — for typography
  • Google Analytics — for anonymous traffic analysis
  • Google AdSense — for advertising (we do not control ad targeting)
  • Google Maps — embedded on contact page

6. Data Security

Since we don't collect or store personal data on our servers, the risk of data breach is minimal. Your calculation history stays in your browser only.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.
